## Rules

## Rebuys

If there are **fewer than 20 players**, rebuys are allowed during the **first hour**, until a total of **$1,000** is on the table.

## Add-Ons

If $1,000 has not been reached after the first hour, players with a stack of **$25 or less** may **add on for $25**.

If there are more add-on requests than the $1,000 limit allows, priority will be given to players with the **smallest stacks**.

## Gameplay Rules

- Standard Texas Hold’em rules only. No 7-2 bonus, silly straights, or other side rules.
- Verbal declarations such as **call, raise, fold, or all-in are binding**. If a player announces one action but moves a different number of chips, the verbal declaration takes precedence.
- If a player attempts to raise by less than the minimum legal raise, the action will count as a call.
- Keep your cards and chips clearly visible at all times.
- Do not discuss the contents of a live hand or give advice while a hand is still being played.
- Act in turn whenever possible. Repeatedly acting out of turn may result in a warning or a penalty of one small blind.
- If a hand reaches showdown, the last player to make an aggressive action must show first. Subsequent players may muck if they are beaten.
- If a hole card is accidentally dealt face up, the player will receive a replacement card, and the exposed card will become the first burn card. If a second card is exposed during the initial deal, the hand will be declared a misdeal and redealt.
- No string bets. A raise must be made in a single, continuous motion. Once chips have been released into the pot, a player may not add additional chips to increase the bet unless the raise was verbally declared beforehand.
- The tournament host has final say in unusual situations, with fairness and consistency taking priority.
